Concepts
========

These pages explain the ideas the rest of the documentation relies on.
The background pages introduce the Linux and platform mechanisms that
shirudo builds on, for readers who have not worked with them. The pages
after them explain shirudo's own model: the operator and target roles,
the objects an operator ships to targets, and the measurement register
that records what a target runs. The glossary defines each term with a
link to the page that explains it.
