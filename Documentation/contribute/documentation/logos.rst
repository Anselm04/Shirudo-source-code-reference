Logos
=====

The shirudo logos for talks, articles, slides and diagrams, and how to
use them and the project name. The logo was designed by Vadim
Shchekoldin.

Variants
--------

Every logo comes in a black version for light backgrounds and a white
one for dark backgrounds, as SVG and as PNG. Prefer the SVG, which
scales to any size, and the horizontal logo wherever it fits.

Use of the name and logos
-------------------------

You can use the shirudo name and logos to refer to the project, for
example in talks, blog posts, articles, slides, course material and
diagrams that show shirudo. You can use them for educational and
instructional purposes, as long as the use is not confusing or
misleading and does not suggest that the project sponsors you or your
work.

- Write the name in lowercase, shirudo, also at the start of a sentence.
- Pick the version made for your background, and the symbol alone only
  where the horizontal or inline logo does not fit.
- Do not modify or alter the official logos: keep their colors and
  proportions, and add nothing to them.
- Do not build a shirudo logo into a logo of your own.
- Do not use the project name, logos or screenshots in ways that could
  be confusing or misleading or imply endorsement.
